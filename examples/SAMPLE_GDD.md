# Sample Stealth Game

## Art Direction

Premium stylized 3D with playful suspense, clean geometric environments, deep midnight blue and purple surfaces, controlled cyan lighting, restrained magenta accents, crisp silhouettes, and strong mobile readability.

## Environment

The first chapter uses a seamless office floor tile and a dark corporate background environment.

## Characters

The player character is a small hooded thief. Guards are readable stylized enemies.

## Props

A security terminal prop appears in office levels.

## UI

The main menu uses a simple panel and button treatment.

## Launch

The game needs a launcher icon and a landscape splash screen.
